# Credits: Zombie Town (Kenney art)

The images in `assets/` are made from three art packs by **Kenney**
(Kenney Vleugels, [kenney.nl](https://www.kenney.nl)), released under
**CC0 1.0** ([Creative Commons Zero, public domain
dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). Credit is
not required by the licence; we give it gladly. Thank you, Kenney.

They are regenerated by `node scripts/import-art.mjs zombie-kenney` from the
recipe `art/imports/zombie-kenney.mjs`, which downloads each zip and checks
its SHA-256 (see [docs/art.md](../../docs/art.md#imported-art)).

## Which file made which asset

| Asset | Made from |
|-------|-----------|
| `road@2x.png` | `isometricCity.zip`: `PNG/cityTiles_080.png` (asphalt), top face resampled to 128×64 |
| `floor@2x.png` (floor and landing) | `isometricCity.zip`: `PNG/cityTiles_072.png` (concrete), top face resampled to 128×64 |
| `grass@2x.png` | `isometricLandscape.zip`: `PNG/landscapeTiles_075.png` (grass), top face resampled to 128×64 |
| `car_s@2x.png` | `isometricVehicles.zip`: `PNG/Civilian/Black/Sedan 1/carBlack2_004.png` at ×3, on the road tile above |
| `car_w@2x.png` | `isometricVehicles.zip`: `PNG/Civilian/Black/Sedan 1/carBlack2_009.png` at ×3, on the road tile above |

This file covers only the images in `packs/zombie-kenney/assets/`. The
rest of the repository's art (the pixel-art SVGs of every other pack, and
the walls, furniture, items and characters this skin leaves alone) is the
project's own and is not covered by it.
